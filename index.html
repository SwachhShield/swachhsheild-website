The idea

Hygiene deserves
better engineering.

SWACHHSHIELD explores how enclosure design, a robotic cleaning mechanism and controlled water delivery could work together in a compact sanitation system. It is a prototype concept under development—not a certified or commercially validated product.

◎

Enclosed by design

A transparent dome concept with suction-cup mounting is intended to help contain splashes during the cleaning cycle.

⌁

Robotic precision

A multi-axis servo-driven arm and brush are proposed to reach cleaning areas with a repeatable motion path.

✳

Smarter cleaning

A downward-facing vision module is proposed for stain detection and adaptive path planning, subject to prototype testing.

Product concept

Purpose-built.
Not an afterthought.

The concept brings together a transparent enclosure, robotic brush, and detergent spray system in a removable assembly intended for Indian-style squat toilets.

The workflow

One concept. Four stages
of product thinking.

A practical development path from digital design through testing. Each stage should be validated before making performance or safety claims.

Design in 3D

Develop component geometry and assembly in CAD, checking clearances and mounting.

Build a prototype

Use suitable materials and 3D-printed parts to evaluate fit and mechanism movement.

Test the system

Evaluate splash containment, brush coverage, water delivery and electrical safety.

Improve with evidence

Use test observations to refine the design before considering wider deployment.
